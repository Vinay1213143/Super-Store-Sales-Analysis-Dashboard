# 📊 Super Store Sales Analytics Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Super Store Sales Analytics Dashboard** is an interactive Power BI project designed to analyze sales performance, profitability, order trends, and regional business performance.

The dashboard transforms sales data into meaningful business insights using interactive visualizations, KPIs, and year-over-year comparisons. It helps users understand sales distribution, identify profitable product categories, and evaluate shipping performance.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall sales, profits, and order volume.
- Compare sales performance across different regions.
- Track monthly sales and profit trends year over year.
- Evaluate sales by customer segment and payment mode.
- Analyze shipping methods and delivery duration.
- Identify top-performing product categories and sub-categories.
- Visualize state-wise sales and profitability.

## 📈 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

| KPI | Description |
|---|---|
| Total Sales | Overall revenue generated |
| Total Orders | Total number of orders |
| Total Profit | Overall profit generated |
| Average Ship Days | Average shipping duration |

## 📊 Dashboard Features

### 1. Sales by Region
Analyzes sales distribution across Central, East, South, and West regions.

### 2. Sales by Customer Segment
Compares sales across Consumer, Corporate, and Home Office segments.

### 3. Sales by Payment Mode
Analyzes customer payment preferences, including online payments, cash on delivery, and cards.

### 4. Monthly Sales and Profit Analysis
Visualizes monthly sales and profit trends with year-over-year comparisons to identify growth patterns and seasonal fluctuations.

### 5. Sales by Ship Mode
Compares sales across Standard Class, Second Class, First Class, and Same Day shipping methods.

### 6. Sales and Profit by State
Displays geographical sales performance across states to identify important markets and regional opportunities.

### 7. Sales by Category
Evaluates the performance of Office Supplies, Technology, and Furniture.

### 8. Sales by Sub-Category
Highlights product-level sales performance to identify leading sub-categories.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Power BI Desktop** – Dashboard development and data visualization
- **Power Query** – Data cleaning and transformation
- **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)** – KPI calculations and analytical measures
- **Data Modeling** – Organizing data and establishing relationships

## 🔍 Business Insights

The dashboard helps answer important business questions:

- Which region contributes the most to total sales?
- Which customer segment generates the highest sales?
- How do sales and profits change month over month and year over year?
- Which shipping method accounts for the largest sales volume?
- Which product categories and sub-categories perform best?
- How does sales performance vary across states?
- Which payment methods are most commonly used by customers?

## 💡 Business Value

This project demonstrates how business intelligence tools can convert raw sales data into actionable insights. The dashboard supports data-driven decision-making by helping businesses monitor performance, understand customer purchasing patterns, and identify opportunities to improve profitability.
